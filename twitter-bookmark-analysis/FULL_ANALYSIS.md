### Tier 2: REAL BUT OVERHYPED — Proceed With Caution

| Strategy | Reality | Adjusted Expectation |
|----------|---------|---------------------|
| Copy-trading | Latency + slippage destroy returns on low-cap | 10-30% returns on larger caps, not 100x |
| Airdrop farming | Sybil detection is killing it | Calculate ROI after gas, time, ban risk |
| Memecoin sniping | 95%+ of participants lose | Treat as gambling with an edge, not income |
| AI/ChatGPT trading bots | Can build basic bots, not $23K/day bots | Useful for automation, not alpha generation |
| Polymarket bots | Arbitrage exists, $50→$25K does not | Small edge possible with proper modeling |

### Tier 3: AVOID — Pure Hype / Scam Vectors

| Red Flag | Why |
|----------|-----|
| "Zero effort" or "no skills needed" | Every profitable strategy requires skill |
| Specific income claims ($30K/week) | Engagement bait, unverifiable |
| "Secret strategy" shared to 100K followers | Not secret. You are the product. |
| "Just copy this whale" | You are the exit liquidity |
| "I built a bot in 20 min with ChatGPT" | Real bots take engineering; 20-min bots lose money |
| Any claim of "100% win rate" | Statistically impossible |
